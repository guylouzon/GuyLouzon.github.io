# Nebius Group (NBIS) - Comprehensive Reverse DCF Analysis

## Executive Summary

Based on a detailed reverse discounted cash flow analysis covering all business segments and core fundamental KPIs, **Nebius Group appears significantly overvalued at current levels**. While the company demonstrates exceptional revenue growth and operates in a rapidly expanding AI infrastructure market, the current stock price of \$107.80 implies expectations that are difficult to justify even under optimistic scenarios[^1_1][^1_2][^1_3].

## Current Financial Position

Nebius Group has delivered remarkable financial performance in recent quarters, with Q2 2025 revenue reaching \$105.1 million, representing 625% year-over-year growth and 106% sequential growth[^1_1][^1_2]. The company raised its 2025 annualized run-rate revenue (ARR) guidance to \$900 million - \$1.1 billion, supported by a significant Microsoft partnership worth \$17.4 billion to \$19.4 billion over five years[^1_4][^1_5].

**Key Financial Metrics (Q2 2025):**

- Revenue: \$105.1M (+625% YoY, +106% QoQ)
- Gross Margin: 71.4% (significant improvement from 46.7% in Q1)
- Operating Margin: -105.8% (heavy investment phase)
- EBITDA Margin: 55.5% (core business achieved positive adjusted EBITDA)
- Market Cap: \$25.9B (239.8M shares outstanding)


## Business Segment Analysis

### Core AI Infrastructure (Nebius AI) - 55.2% of Revenue

The flagship AI cloud infrastructure business generated \$68.3 million in 2024, representing the primary growth driver[^1_6]. The segment operates GPU clusters optimized for AI training and inference, achieving near-peak utilization rates. The company is rapidly expanding capacity from 220 megawatts by end-2025 to over 1 gigawatt by 2026[^1_1][^1_7].

**Key Developments:**

- Microsoft partnership provides dedicated capacity from New Jersey data center
- Expansion into multiple geographic markets (Finland, UK, Israel, Iceland)
- Purpose-built infrastructure optimized for AI workloads
- Strong customer traction with enterprises and AI startups


### Educational Technology (TripleTen) - 23.3% of Revenue

TripleTen contributed \$28.8 million in 2024 revenue, offering AI-enhanced bootcamps in software engineering, cybersecurity, and data science[^1_8]. The business is approaching breakeven with over 14,000 new learners in 2024 and expansion into Latin America.

### Data Services (Toloka) - 21.3% of Revenue

The mature data annotation business generated \$26.4 million in 2024, providing AI training data services that complement the core infrastructure offering[^1_6].

### Autonomous Systems (Avride) - 0.2% of Revenue

The early-stage autonomous delivery business completed over 200,000 deliveries and shows high growth potential, though currently represents minimal revenue contribution[^1_8].

## Core Fundamental KPIs

### Profitability and Return Metrics

- **ROIC (TTM): -18.7%** - Negative due to heavy investment phase and operating losses[^1_9][^1_10]
- **ROE: 6.0%** - Reasonable for a growth-stage company[^1_11]
- **ROA: 5.0%** - Reflecting expanding asset base[^1_11]
- **ROCE: -10.0%** - Negative operating income impacts return on capital[^1_12]


### Margin Evolution

The company demonstrates strong gross margin expansion potential, improving from 46.7% in Q1 2025 to 71.4% in Q2 2025[^1_1]. However, operating margins remain deeply negative at -105.8% due to aggressive capacity expansion and R\&D investments.

**Projected Margin Trajectory:**

- Gross margins expected to stabilize at 70-72% as operations mature
- Operating margins projected to turn positive by 2028, reaching 25%+ by 2033
- Path to profitability dependent on successful scaling and operational leverage


## Growth Projections and Expected Returns

### Revenue Growth Expectations

Based on current guidance and market opportunities, revenue is projected to grow from \$630 million in 2025 to over \$21 billion by 2035, driven by:

- **Microsoft Contract Impact**: \$3.5-4 billion annual contribution starting 2026
- **Capacity Expansion**: 1+ gigawatt target creates significant scale advantages
- **Market Growth**: AI infrastructure market expanding at 28.5% CAGR[^1_13]
- **Geographic Expansion**: International data center rollout


### Free Cash Flow Analysis

The company is expected to burn cash through 2028 due to heavy capital expenditure requirements (\$13+ billion cumulative through 2035). Free cash flow should turn positive by 2029, reaching \$3.4 billion by 2035 as operational leverage materializes.

## Reverse DCF Valuation Results

### Base Case Scenario

Using a 14.0% WACC and 3.0% terminal growth rate, the DCF analysis yields a fair value of **\$56.94 per share**, implying **47.2% downside** from current levels of \$107.80.

**Key Valuation Inputs:**

- Enterprise Value: \$12.14 billion
- Terminal FCF (2035): \$3.5 billion
- Net Cash Position: \$1.51 billion
- WACC: 14.0% (reflecting high beta and execution risk)


### Sensitivity Analysis

- **Conservative Case**: \$40.47 per share (-62.5% vs current)
- **Base Case**: \$56.94 per share (-47.2% vs current)
- **Optimistic Case**: \$66.79 per share (-38.0% vs current)

Even under optimistic assumptions with accelerated growth and higher terminal margins, the stock appears overvalued at current levels.

## Investment Risks and Opportunities

### Key Risks

- **Capital Intensity**: \$13+ billion capex requirements over the next decade strain financial resources
- **Competition**: Hyperscalers (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) expanding GPU offerings with improved networking and bare-metal options[^1_14]
- **Execution Risk**: Ambitious 1GW capacity expansion requires flawless execution
- **Customer Concentration**: Heavy dependence on Microsoft contract creates concentration risk
- **GPU Supply**: Reliance on Nvidia GPU supply chain introduces potential bottlenecks


### Strategic Opportunities

- **Market Leadership**: First-mover advantage in specialized AI cloud infrastructure
- **Revenue Visibility**: \$19.4 billion Microsoft contract provides near-term cash flow certainty
- **Operational Leverage**: Path to 25%+ operating margins as scale economies emerge
- **Geographic Expansion**: International footprint expansion opportunities
- **Software Monetization**: Higher-margin software and services revenue potential


## Conclusion

While Nebius Group operates at the center of the AI infrastructure revolution and demonstrates exceptional revenue growth, the current valuation appears disconnected from fundamental value. The stock price implies near-perfect execution of an extremely aggressive growth scenario that may be difficult to achieve given competitive dynamics and capital requirements.

**Investment Recommendation: Overvalued** - The reverse DCF analysis suggests fair value ranges from \$40-67 per share across various scenarios, indicating significant downside risk from current levels above \$100. Investors should wait for a more attractive entry point or look for companies with better risk-adjusted return profiles in the AI infrastructure space.
