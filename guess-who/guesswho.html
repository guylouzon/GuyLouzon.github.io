Guess Who?

Game Features

  • Random Character Selection: The game randomly selects two characters - one for you and one for your opponent.
  • Character Board: Displays a grid of all available character images that you can click to eliminate/restore.
  • Mystery Card: Shows your assigned character at the top of the screen.
  • Link Sharing: Generates a link for your opponent with the characters swapped so they see their character but not yours.

How to Play

  1. Start a new game - the app will select a random character for you and your opponent
  2. Click "Generate Link for Opponent" and share that link with your friend
  3. Ask yes/no questions about your opponent's character
  4. Click on characters to eliminate ones that don't match the answers
  5. Continue until you can guess which character your opponent has
